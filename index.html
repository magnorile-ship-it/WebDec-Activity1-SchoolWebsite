Junior Highschool (JHS)

The core purpose of Junior High School (Grades 7 to 10) is to serve as a critical bridge between elementary education and Senior High School, providing a formative foundation for adolescent learners.

Senior Highschool (SHS)

The purpose of Senior High School (Grades 11 and 12) is to bridge basic education and real-world success by preparing students for higher education, direct employment, middle-level skills development, or entrepreneurship.
